# Simulation Results: Quadratic Voting

**Mechanism**: VP_new = sqrt(VP_old)

Quadratic voting reduces the marginal influence of each additional token,
equalizing power between large and small holders. A voter with 10,000 tokens
gets VP=100, while a voter with 1 token gets VP=1 — a 100x ratio instead of 10,000x.

## Summary

Quadratic voting (VP = √VP) reduces concentration across all metrics.
The transformation compresses the VP distribution, making large holders
less dominant while preserving the ordering of voter influence.
