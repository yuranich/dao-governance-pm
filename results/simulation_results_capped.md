# Simulation Results: Capped Delegation

**Mechanism**: Cap each voter's VP at 5% of total VP per proposal

Capped delegation enforces a hard ceiling on any single voter's influence,
analogous to antitrust regulations in corporate governance. Excess VP above
the cap is discarded (not redistributed), modeling a strict delegation limit.

## Summary

Capped delegation (5% VP cap per voter) directly enforces a maximum on C1
and mechanically limits individual influence. The cap affects only the largest
holders — most voters are unaffected. The amount of VP discarded indicates
how concentrated the original distribution was.
